# Grill Me

A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design.

## Rules

- Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for the answer before asking the next.
- Never accept "it depends" without a follow-up: "depends on what?"
- Surface the riskiest assumption first.
- When the user says "probably" or "I think", ask what it would take to know for sure.
- When the user proposes a solution, ask what problem it solves, then ask if there's a simpler way to solve that problem.
- Keep going until there are no major open questions.

## Useful angles for SAFE project

- **Validity**: Are the 30 scenarios enough to show statistical significance?
- **Confounds**: What other variables besides A/B could explain the differences?
- **Generalizability**: Does the lab setup reflect real attacker behavior?
- **Stability**: How do you handle Ollama timeouts in the analysis without biasing toward Condición A?
- **Reproducibility**: Can Mario reproduce the full setup from the instructivo alone?
